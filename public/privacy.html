Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

BioPicker is made by ePerf. This policy explains what we collect when you use BioPicker on the web or on iPhone, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your account: a username, a password (stored only as a one-way hash) and your authenticator setup. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • What you add to a biopic: photos (including the date and place they were taken, if the photo records them), documents, timeline events, interview answers, music and the details you type about the person.
  • What BioPicker makes: scripts, narration and films.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications in the iPhone app, a token so we can tell you when a film is ready.
  • Server logs: IP addresses and request times, kept briefly for security and to stop abuse.

How it's used

Only to make your biopics and run your account. To write questions, research history, cast a narrator and write scripts, the text, documents and photos in a biopic are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which processes them on our behalf. Narration text is sent to a text-to-speech service (Microsoft Edge voices, or ElevenLabs when enabled). Place names, and the coordinates recorded in photos, are looked up with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to show places on maps. Public-domain archival media is fetched from public collections. We don't sell your data, show ads or use your content to train AI models.

Who can see it

Your biopics are private to your account. If you make a biopic public, anyone with its link can watch its latest films and see thumbnails of its included photos; everything else stays private. You can make it private again at any time.

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleted biopics stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, then are erased. Deleting your account (Account settings → Delete account) erases your account and all of its biopics immediately.
