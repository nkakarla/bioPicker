BioPicker

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

BioPicker is made by EPerf. This policy explains what we collect when you use BioPicker on the web or on iPhone, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your account: a username and a password (stored only as a one-way hash). We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • What you add to a biopic: photos (including the date and place they were taken, if the photo records them), documents, timeline events, interview answers, music and the details you type about the person.
  • What BioPicker makes: scripts, narration and films.
  • Usage records: how much AI work and how many film renders your account uses each month (counts and times, not the content), to run plans and limits.
  • Where you sign in from: when you create your account and each time you sign in, the IP address, the approximate place it points to (city, region and country), your device and browser type, and when you sign up, the web page that linked you to BioPicker (if any). We keep the sign-up details and your last 10 sign-ins with your account, to prevent abuse (such as one person making many free accounts) and to understand where BioPicker is used. The place is looked up on our own server with the DB-IP database; your IP address isn't sent to anyone else for this.
  • Payments: if you buy a plan, which plan, its status and renewal date, and an id from the payment service. On the website, card payments are handled by Stripe; in the iPhone app, by Apple. We never see or store your card details.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications in the iPhone app, a token so we can tell you when a film is ready.
  • Server logs: IP addresses and request times, kept briefly for security and to stop abuse.

How it's used

To make your biopics, run your account, and keep BioPicker safe and working well. To write questions, research history, cast a narrator and write scripts, the text, documents and photos in a biopic are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which processes them on our behalf. Narration text is sent to a text-to-speech service (Microsoft Edge voices, or ElevenLabs when enabled). Place names, and the coordinates recorded in photos, are looked up with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to show places on maps. Public-domain archival media is fetched from public collections. We don't sell your data, show ads or use your content to train AI models.

Who can see it

Your biopics are private to your account, with one exception: the people who run BioPicker at EPerf can view any biopic and its films, including private ones, to check that BioPicker isn’t being used for unlawful or harmful material, to look into reports and problems, and to understand what people make so we can improve it. Each time they open a biopic this way it is recorded. They don’t share what they see. If you make a biopic public, anyone with its link can watch its latest films and see thumbnails of its included photos; everything else stays private. You can make it private again at any time.

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleted biopics stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, then are erased. Deleting your account (Account settings → Delete account) erases your account, its sign-in records and all of its biopics immediately. A subscription paid on the website is cancelled at the same time; one paid through Apple must be cancelled in your App Store subscriptions. Stripe and Apple keep their own payment records as the law requires.
